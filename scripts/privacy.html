CDD Stoich Tools

Privacy policy

The short version: the extension works inside your browser on CDD Vault pages and sends nothing anywhere.

What's new Source on GitHub

Where it runs

Only on pages under collaborativedrug.com, the CDD Vault web application. It does not load on any other site and has no access to other tabs.

What it reads

The extension reads the same data CDD Vault already sends to your browser for the page you have open — ELN entries, samples, batches, locations, plates — in order to show it in the sample panel, copy it to your clipboard, print it, or fill in a CDD form for you. It acts under your own CDD login and can do nothing your account cannot.

What it stores

Your settings: which panel fields you show, prefix colours, the width of a panel, saved phrases, and similar preferences. They are kept with the browser's extension storage (the only permission the extension asks for) and stay on your computer. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

What leaves your browser

Nothing. There is no backend, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account, and no remote code. The only network requests the extension makes are to the CDD Vault server you are already logged into, on your behalf, for actions you triggered — the same requests the CDD page itself would make.

Not a CDD product

CDD Stoich Tools is an open-source extension written by users of CDD Vault. Collaborative Drug Discovery does not build, endorse, or support it, and has no access to anything through it.
