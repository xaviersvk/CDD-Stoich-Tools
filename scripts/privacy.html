CDD Stoich Tools

Privacy policy

The short version: the extension works inside your browser on CDD Vault pages and sends nothing anywhere — unless you switch on the optional AI write-up, which sends the text you ask it to polish to CERIT-SC, and never a structure.

What's new Source on GitHub

Where it runs

Only on pages under collaborativedrug.com, the CDD Vault web application. It does not load on any other site and has no access to other tabs. With AI write-up switched on it also talks to one more server, llm.ai.e-infra.cz — see below.

What it reads

The extension reads the same data CDD Vault already sends to your browser for the page you have open — ELN entries, samples, batches, locations, plates — in order to show it in the sample panel, copy it to your clipboard, print it, or fill in a CDD form for you. It acts under your own CDD login and can do nothing your account cannot.

What it stores

Your settings: which panel fields you show, prefix colours, the width of a panel, saved phrases, and similar preferences — and, if you use AI write-up, your CERIT API key and house rules. They are kept with the browser's extension storage and stay on your computer. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

What leaves your browser

By default, nothing. There is no backend, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account of ours, and no remote code. The extension's requests go to the CDD Vault server you are already logged into, on your behalf, for actions you triggered — the same requests the CDD page itself would make. The one exception is AI write-up, below, and only while you have it switched on.

AI write-up (optional, off by default)

AI write-up polishes ELN text into English and checks it against the stoichiometry table, using the language models of CERIT-SC AI as a Service (llm.ai.e-infra.cz, run by Masaryk University within e-INFRA CZ) under your own CERIT account and API key. Switching it on asks the browser for access to that one server; switching it off gives the access back.

What is sent, and only when you click 🎤, Polish or Polish entry: the text of the ELN entry (or the part you selected), the stoichiometry table as compound names, amounts and equivalents, the names of records the entry links to, your house rules, and — for dictation — the audio you recorded.

What is never sent: structures in any form — no SMILES, mol or MRV files, reaction images or molecular weights — nor attached files, nor your CDD login. The API key goes only to CERIT.

What CERIT does with requests is governed by CERIT-SC's own terms and AI data policy. The answers are shown in the extension's panel for you to copy; nothing is written into your ELN entry.

Not a CDD product

CDD Stoich Tools is an open-source extension written by users of CDD Vault. Collaborative Drug Discovery does not build, endorse, or support it, and has no access to anything through it.
